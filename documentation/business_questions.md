Business Questions

The analysis was structured around business questions rather than isolated technical queries.

Support Demand

Which issue categories generate the highest volume of support tickets?

Which support priorities account for the greatest ticket volume?

Which customer contact channels are used most frequently?

How does ticket volume change month by month?

Operational Performance

Which issue categories take the longest to resolve?

Which priority levels have the longest average resolution times?

Which issue categories have the highest escalation rates?

Which support channels have the slowest average first-response times?

How does average resolution time change over time?

SLA Performance

What proportion of tickets breach the SLA?

Which priority levels have the highest SLA breach rates?

Which priority levels represent the greatest operational SLA risk?

Customer Experience

What is the average customer satisfaction score among rated tickets?

How does CSAT vary by issue category?

How do operational measures such as resolution time and escalation relate to areas with lower CSAT?

Management Use

Which support areas should be investigated first based on the combination of ticket volume, resolution time, escalation, SLA risk, and CSAT?

What operational improvements could be considered based on the observed support patterns?

These questions guided the Excel analysis, SQL queries, Power BI dashboard, insights, and recommendations.
