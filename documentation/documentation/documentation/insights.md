Business Insights

1. Support demand is concentrated in Technical and Account & Login issues

Technical generated 1,944 tickets and Account & Login generated 1,929 tickets. Together they account for 3,873 tickets, or 38.73% of the 10,000-ticket dataset.

This indicates that these two areas represent a substantial share of support demand and may warrant investigation into recurring issues, self-service opportunities, troubleshooting guidance, or product improvements.

2. Integration shows the strongest operational pressure

Integration tickets have the highest average resolution time at 18.22 hours and the highest escalation rate at 31.85% among the seven issue categories.

Integration also has the lowest category-level CSAT at 3.96/5.

These measures identify Integration as an area requiring further investigation. The dataset does not establish that slower resolution or escalation causes lower CSAT.

Overall Finding

The strongest operational signals in the dataset are concentrated around complex support areas, high-priority SLA performance, response-time differences across channels, and increasing ticket demand.

These findings provide a basis for targeted operational investigation and business recommendations rather than definitive causal conclusions.
